## Invitation and purpose

You are invited to take part in a research study about how computer systems remember information across conversations over a short period. Read this page and ask questions before deciding. Taking part is voluntary. You may stop using the study assistant at any time without penalty, subject to the approved compensation terms below.

## What participation involves

If you agree, you will use a text-based study assistant for your own ordinary, self-chosen needs during one seven-day period. We do not assign conversation topics or ask you to share particular facts. Please do not enter passwords, financial account information, exact home or work addresses, medical details, secrets, or identifying information about other people. The study assistant's cross-session memory is disabled during collection. We plan to collect the text messages you send and receive, UTC timestamps, and session boundaries so researchers can replay conversations for memory-system evaluation. We do not plan to collect audio, location, contacts, uploaded files, or web/tool histories.

At the end, researchers may create questions about information that appears in your conversation. You will not be evaluated or judged personally. The study evaluates computer systems. The target is approximately 16 questions per completed episode, including some questions that cannot be answered from the transcript.

## Data handling and risks

Conversation text can contain personal or sensitive information even when we ask you not to enter it. Authorized study staff will review and redact direct identifiers and sensitive incidental content. Other people may be mentioned in a conversation; we will minimize or remove identifying details about them. There remains a risk that a person could recognize a de-identified conversation from its context. We cannot promise zero re-identification risk.

Raw records will be stored [describe encryption, location, access roles, backup, retention and deletion schedule]. Researchers who annotate test questions will receive only the minimum text needed for their task. Public development transcripts may be made available on Hugging Face and GitHub only if you separately opt in below and your record passes privacy and rights review. Hidden-test transcripts and answers will be kept in access-controlled storage for [retention period] and used by authorized evaluators under [access policy]. No identifying linkage file will be published.

Research data may be used to compare conversational-memory systems. The benchmark is not designed to evaluate medical, legal, financial, or other high-stakes advice. Published results may include aggregate statistics and de-identified text only as described in your choices below. We will not sell your transcript.
